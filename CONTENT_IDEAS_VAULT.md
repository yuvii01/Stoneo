## 1. Viral Short-Form Hooks (Reels / Shorts / TikTok)
*Designed for high retention, immediate visual disruption, and high replay rates (15–45 seconds).*

1. **The Dark-to-Light Switch (The Glow Reveal):**
   * *Visual:* Camera rests on a dark, mysterious textured surface. Hands flip a switch $\rightarrow$ warm backlighting explodes through Blue Agate or Wild Amber Agate.
   * *Hook:* *"Why ultra-luxury penthouses are ditching marble for glowing stones."*
2. **The "Wet vs. Dry" Transformation:**
   * *Visual:* A dull, dry unpolished semi-precious slab gets misted with water or buffed with finishing oil to show the sudden crystalline depth pop.
3. **ASMR Stone Tap & Squeegee:**
   * *Visual:* Zero talking. Macro microphone capturing the sound of a ring tapping the stone, diamond pad polishing, water squeegee sliding over mirror-finish resin.
4. **The Scratch & Durability Test:**
   * *Visual:* Aggressively dragging a sharp stainless steel knife, keys, and coins across a polished Agate slab.
   * *Hook:* *"Is a ₹1,00,000 stone table actually scratch-proof?"*
5. **The Curry & Lemon Acid Test:**
   * *Visual:* Pouring lemon juice, wine, and turmeric curry on Italian marble vs. semi-precious agate composite. Marble etches; agate wipes clean.
6. **"Guess the Price" Game:**
   * *Visual:* Showcase an 8-seater Tiger Eye dining table or Backlit Amethyst bar with close-up shots. Display a countdown timer for comments before revealing the actual cost.
7. **Daylight vs. Artificial Backlighting:**
   * *Visual:* Split-screen comparison showing how translucent Rose Quartz or White Quartz behaves under natural sunlight vs. warm 3000K LED light sheets.
8. **The "Rich People Things" Trend:**
   * *Hook:* *"Things in multi-millionaire homes that just make sense: Part 1 - Backlit Gemstone Walls."*
9. **Unboxing an Imported Gemstone Slab:**
   * *Visual:* Prying open heavy wooden export crates filled with vacuum-wrapped semi-precious slabs shipped from Italy or Madagascar.
10. **The Magnifying / Macro Lens Zoom:**
    * *Visual:* Starting 2 inches away from a tiny geode crystal cavity, zooming back 15 feet to reveal it’s an entire feature wall.

## 5. Buyer Guides, Pricing & "Real vs. Fake"
*Builds strong trust and attracts high-intent buying leads.*

35. **Why Are Semi-Precious Stones So Expensive?**
    * A no-BS breakdown: raw mineral scarcity, international freight weight, 40+ hours of hand curation, and specialized fabrication.
36. **Price Guide Per Square Foot:**
    * Realistic price brackets: Entry-level quartz composites vs. Mid-tier agates vs. Ultra-rare malachite and lapis lazuli.
37. **How to Spot Fake / Resin-Printed Stone:**
    * *Test 1:* Thermal touch test (natural minerals stay cold; printed acrylic feels room temp).
    * *Test 2:* Repeating patterns (nature never makes two identical agate rings).
    * *Test 3:* Scratch test on unpolished edges.
38. **The 5 Biggest Installation Mistakes Architects Make:**
    * Leaving no access door for LED drivers, inadequate heat dissipation, and using acidic silicone glues.
39. **Maintenance Guide:**
    * Daily cleaning protocols: what cleaners damage epoxy resin vs. what keeps the mirror shine for decades.
40. **Semi-Precious vs. Translucent Onyx Showdown:**

```
[0:00 - 0:05] HOOK:
Visual: Holding two small stone samples that look almost identical.
Voiceover: "One of these is a ₹20,000 real gemstone slab, and the other is a ₹500 plastic print. Here is how you never get scammed."

[0:05 - 0:25] STEP 1 & 2:
Visual: Touching the surface with a digital infrared thermometer.
Voiceover: "First: The Touch Test. Natural minerals have high thermal conductivity and stay icy cold. Plastic warms up immediately."
Visual: Magnifying glass over both.
Voiceover: "Second: Look for identical rings. If you see the exact same geode circle repeated three times, it's an inkjet print."

[0:25 - 0:45] STEP 3:
Visual: Shining a small UV torch or scratching with a coin.
Voiceover: "Third: Real agate is Mohs hardness 7. A steel coin or knife won't leave a single mark."

[0:45 - 0:60] OUTRO:
Voiceover: "Save this video before your next interior shopping trip, and follow for more luxury stone secrets."
```
